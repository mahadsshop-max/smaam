How it works
1

Set one budget

Pick a total for the day. Spend it on any apps you like. When it hits zero, everything locks until tomorrow.

2

Wake up on camera

Your alarm gives you a task: show your fridge, a glass of water, a tidy desk, or dance for 15 seconds. Film it to switch it off.

3

Keep the streak

Every verified morning adds a tally mark. Miss a day and the count starts over.
